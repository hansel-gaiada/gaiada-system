## The rule that governs the replacement

When the persona program lands, **this file becomes a rendering of the router seat's persona pack —
not a second, independently-edited definition.** Zedano is the `router` seat; its soul and its pack
are one artifact with two consumers.

And the constraint that does not move: **a persona is presentation, never permission.** Nothing
written here may widen what Zedano can do. Capability lives in `agent_registry` and Cerbos; the soul
may only *describe* a limit it is already bound by.
