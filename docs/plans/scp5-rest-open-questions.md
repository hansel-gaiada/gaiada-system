# SCP-5 rest (5b, 5c, 5e to 5h): open questions for the owner

Draft, 2026-10-30. The contract (FRONTEND-BFF-CONTRACT §63.24 to §63.29) is written on the default, the first option of each
line, and marks every place a different ruling would change with "(SCP-Qnn, default)". Nothing here is ruled yet, so none of
it goes into the plan's §2 until the owner answers.

1. **SCP-Q25 (5b) Who sends the weekly report?** (a) default: as the monthly: the officer writes and submits it, one of the four checks and sends it to the client's portal reports; (b) the officer sends it, with no HoD check; (c) internal only, never sent.
2. **SCP-Q26 (5b) Which clients get a weekly report?** (a) default: the clients switched on in the allocation (off until set); (b) every active client; (c) only clients whose contract scope lists it.
3. **SCP-Q27 (5c) Do ideas reach the client?** (a) default: no; "Mark shared" records that the team shared one; (b) shared ideas also print in the next monthly report; (c) the client sees and accepts them in the portal.
4. **SCP-Q28 (5e) Saved captions, templates and references:** (a) default: per client, as the team's prototype; (b) templates team-wide, the rest per client; (c) all team-wide, with an optional client.
5. **SCP-Q29 (5e) Who curates the content bank?** (a) default: the Social Media team adds; changing or deleting someone else's item is the client's officer, the HoD or the GM; (b) only the client's officer and the four; (c) anyone who reads social, Creatives included.
6. **SCP-Q30 (5e) Videos in the content bank:** (a) default: Drive links only (the Asset Library stores images); (b) a separate ticket to store video files in the ERP.
7. **SCP-Q31 (5f) Which monthly-cycle steps close themselves?** (a) default: all six, each on its pipeline condition; (b) only artwork, client approval and scheduled; report, planning and copy lock are ticked by hand; (c) none: the pipeline shows progress and people tick.
8. **SCP-Q32 (5g) The audit's twelve dimensions, weights, bands and benchmark:** (a) default: the 15 Oct twelve, reworded, equal weights, bands at 75 and 50, the benchmark our own clients' median engagement rate by business type over 90 days, all reviewed with the team at SCP-7d; (b) the team sends its list and numbers before the build; (c) an industry benchmark table the four type in Settings.
9. **SCP-Q33 (5g) Where does a prospect live?** (a) default: a name and a business type on the audit itself, attached to the client once signed; (b) a lead in the agency discovery intake (`agency_leads`); (c) a client row in a new prospect status.
10. **SCP-Q34 (5g) Who writes audits?** (a) default: a client's audit by its officer, the HoD or the GM; a prospect's by the four; (b) the four only; (c) any member of the team.
11. **SCP-Q35 (5h) The onboarding checklist:** (a) default: the team's fifteen steps in six phases, reworded, seeded as a PM project template the four edit, with placeholder offsets the team sets; (b) the team sends its final list and offsets before the build; (c) a blank template the four fill in.
12. **SCP-Q36 (5h) Who starts a client's onboarding?** (a) default: the four (it hands tasks to people); (b) the four and the client's officer.
